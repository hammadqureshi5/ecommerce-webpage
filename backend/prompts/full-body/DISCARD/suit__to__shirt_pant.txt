build whichever of these two constructions it actually shows. A pull-on waist is
one smooth continuous band of the same cloth, softly gathered all the way round
and closed by a drawstring whose two ends hang at the centre front. A denim or
tailored waist is a flat firm band with a fly, a button or hook closure, and
belt loops spaced evenly around it. Draw one or the other and never parts of
both, and match the number and spacing of any loops. If the top covers the
waistband in the reference so that you cannot see how it is built, let the top
cover it the same way here and leave the waist plain, rather than inventing a
drawstring, a buckle or loops to fill the gap.

buttoned cuff. Do not lengthen a short sleeve or shorten a long one, and do not
change it to suit the weather, the setting or the rest of the outfit.
